Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2024

Home / Privacy Policy

1. Introduction

Beijing Hongchangyuan Travel Service Co., Ltd. ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website bjhongchangyuan.com, use our mobile applications, or utilize our services.

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. By accessing or using our services, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agree to be bound by all the terms of this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Personal Information

We may collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us, including but not limited to:

  • Name, email address, phone number, and postal address
  • Date of birth and demographic information
  • Payment information and financial details
  • Travel preferences, passport information, and visa details
  • Company information for corporate clients
  • Any other information you choose to provide

2.2 Automatically Collected Information

When you visit our website or use our mobile applications, we automatically collect certain information, including:

  • Device type, operating system, and browser information
  • IP address and device identifiers
  • Usage patterns and browsing behavior
  • Location data (with your consent)
  • Cookies and similar tracking technologies

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve our services
  • Process bookings, reservations, and payments
  • Communicate with you about our services and updates
  • Personalize your experience and tailor content
  • Send promotional communications (with your consent)
  • Ensure security and prevent fraud
  • Comply with legal obligations

4. Information Sharing and Disclosure

We may share your information with:

  • Service providers who assist in our operations (hotels, airlines, payment processors)
  • Business partners with your consent
  • Legal authorities when required by law
  • Third parties in connection with corporate transactions (mergers, acquisitions)

5. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is 100% secure.

6. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access your personal information
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Object to or restrict certain processing
  • Data portability
  • Withdraw consent at any time

7. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We use cookies and similar tracking technologies to collect information about your browsing activities. You can control cookies through your browser settings. For more information, please see our Cookie Policy.

8. Children's Privacy

Our services are not intended for individuals under the age of 18 (or the applicable age of consent in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children without parental consent. If you believe we have collected such information, please contact us immediately.

9. International Data Transfers

Your information may be transferred to and processed in countries other than your country of residence. We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place for such transfers in compliance with applicable data protection laws.

10. Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy, unless a longer retention period is required by law. When information is no longer needed, we securely delete or anonymize it.

11. California Privacy Rights (CCPA)

If you are a California resident, you have specific rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA):

  • Right to know what personal information is collected
  • Right to know how personal information is used and shared
  • Right to delete personal information
  • Right to opt-out of the sale of personal information
  • Right to non-discrimination for exercising your rights

12. European Union Data Protection (GDPR)

For users in the European Union, we process your personal data under the following legal bases:

  • Performance of a contract with you
  • Our legitimate business interests
  • Your consent
  • Compliance with legal obligations

You have additional rights under GDPR, including the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.

13.3 Ad Targeting and Personalization

Advertisements in our apps may be personalized based on:

  • Device information and identifiers (GAID, IDFA)
  • App usage patterns and preferences
  • Location data (with your permission)
  • General demographic information
  • Aggregated and anonymized data

You can opt out of personalized advertising through your device settings or the opt-out mechanisms provided by ad networks.

13.4 Age Restrictions for Apps

Our applications are intended for users who are:

  • 18 years of age or older in most countries
  • 16 years of age or older in the European Economic Area (EEA)
  • 13 years of age or older in the United States (COPPA compliance)

If you are under the applicable age, please do not use our applications without parental consent. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children without appropriate parental verification.

13.5 App Store Compliance

Google Play Store Compliance:

  • Our apps comply with Google Play's Developer Program Policies
  • We declare all required permissions and their purposes
  • Age rating: Everyone or Teen (based on content)
  • Data safety forms are completed accurately
  • We provide clear information about ads in the app listing

Apple App Store Compliance:

  • Our apps comply with Apple's App Store Review Guidelines
  • All ATT (App Tracking Transparency) prompts are implemented correctly
  • Age rating: 4+ or 12+ (based on content)
  • We respect user choices regarding tracking permissions
  • Privacy nutrition labels are accurately completed

13.6 Ad-Related Data Collection

Our advertising partners may collect and use the following types of data:

  • Device identifiers (GAID, IDFA, OAID)
  • IP address and network information
  • App usage and interaction data
  • Advertising engagement metrics
  • In-app purchase data (for ad attribution)
  • Location data (for geo-targeted ads, with consent)
  • Cookie data and similar identifiers

This data may be used to deliver relevant advertisements, measure ad effectiveness, and improve ad targeting. For more details, please review the privacy policies of our advertising partners.

14. China's Personal Information Protection Law (PIPL)

For users in China, we comply with the Personal Information Protection Law (PIPL) and related regulations:

  • We collect personal information only with your consent
  • You have the right to access, correct, delete, and port your data
  • We implement necessary security measures to protect your information
  • Cross-border data transfers comply with PIPL requirements
  • We provide clear notices about data processing activities

15. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
