Our Services

Comprehensive travel solutions and corporate event management services tailored to meet your every need.

Core Services

Complete Travel Solutions

From initial planning to execution, we provide end-to-end services for all your travel and event requirements.

01

Travel Project Consulting

Professional consultation services for travel project planning, feasibility studies, market analysis, and strategic planning. Our experts help you make informed decisions for successful travel initiatives.

02

Passenger Ticket Agency

Comprehensive ticket booking services for flights, high-speed trains, buses, and other transportation. We offer competitive rates and flexible booking options for individual and corporate clients.

03

Hotel Booking Services

Access to a global network of partner hotels ranging from budget-friendly options to luxury resorts. We handle reservations, special requests, and group bookings with personalized service.

04

Conference & Exhibition Services

End-to-end conference and exhibition organization including venue selection, program design, speaker coordination, registration management, and on-site support for seamless corporate events.

05

Corporate Team Building

Innovative team building programs designed to enhance collaboration, improve communication, and boost employee engagement. Activities include outdoor adventures, workshops, and custom corporate games.

06

Cultural Exchange Programs

Organizing cultural exchange activities, art exhibitions, performances, and educational programs that foster cross-cultural understanding and international cooperation.

07

Scenic Area Management

Professional scenic area management services including visitor services, facility operations, environmental protection, and tourism experience optimization for attractions and destinations.

08

Business Information Consulting

Expert business consulting services (excluding licensed permits) providing market insights, industry analysis, and strategic recommendations for travel and hospitality businesses.

09

Marketing Planning

Comprehensive marketing strategy development and campaign planning to promote your travel services, events, and destination offerings to target audiences effectively.
